🎯 Topbar Typography Rules:

  • Active state: Change text color to #1D4ED8, background to white
  • Hover state: Background changes to #E3ECFC (lighter tint), no color change
  • Dark mode: Background #18181B, text #D4D4D8, active text #60A5FA
